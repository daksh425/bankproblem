# Content plan

Eight silos, covering all of banking. Breadth is only a liability if the site
reads as a grab-bag, so each silo is built out as a tight cluster with heavy
internal linking before the next one starts. You rank cluster by cluster.

**Order matters.** Commercial silos first: they fund the rest. Support silos
feed them internal links and AdSense impressions, and they are much easier to
rank, so they are good second-year work.

| Silo | Intent | Why | Build order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Credit score | commercial | Best-paying affiliate category in Indian banking. Every answer depends on the reader's own numbers, so it resists AI Overviews. | 1 |
| Cards | commercial | In-market readers; card approvals pay ₹1,500–3,500. | 2 |
| Escalation | support | The keystone. Every other silo links here, which concentrates internal authority on one page. | 3 |
| Fraud | support | Highest urgency on the site. Earns links from forums and consumer groups. | 4 |
| Loans | commercial | Rejection and foreclosure queries have real commercial intent. | 5 |
| NRI | commercial | Highest earning per visitor of anything researched — foreign traffic pays multiples of India traffic. Needs a CA review for tax pages. | 6 |
| Payments | support | Big volume, little money, heavy AI Overview exposure. Worth having, never worth leading with. | 7 |
| Accounts | support | Steady volume, low competition, low value. Filler that supports the rest. | 8 |

**Forty articles ship with the repo**, five in every silo: seven `guide` pages
carrying RBI citations, and thirty-three `post` pages. Every silo is linked into
the escalation silo, and the escalation guide is linked from almost everything —
that is deliberate, and it concentrates internal authority on the pages that
matter.

## The rule for picking a page

Write the page only if you can answer **all four**:

1. Can you state the answer in two sentences? If not, you do not know it yet.
2. Is there a primary source — circular, master direction, statute — that settles it?
3. Does the answer depend on the reader's own situation? If it is one flat fact, Google will answer it in the results page and you will get no click.
4. Does the top of the current SERP contain at least one page that is not a bank, an OTA or a government portal? If every result is an incumbent, pick a narrower question.

Question 3 is the one that matters most now. Prefer **"my loan was rejected
despite a 780 score"** over **"what is a good CIBIL score"**. The first branches
on the reader's circumstances; the second is a number Google will print without
sending anyone to you.

## Cadence

Thirty articles in month one is the plan's figure and it is too many for one
person writing sourced YMYL content. **Ten to twelve a month, properly sourced,
beats thirty thin ones** — and thin informational content is exactly what both
AI Overviews and Google's spam updates are removing.

Finish the credit-score silo before starting cards. A complete cluster of 8
ranks better than 8 orphans spread across 8 topics.
