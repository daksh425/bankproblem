Almost every page on this site ends here, because the Ombudsman is the only free
lever an ordinary customer has over a bank that has stopped listening. It is also
badly understood: people file too early, file about things it does not cover, or
never file at all because they assume it needs a lawyer.

It does not. It takes about twenty minutes online and costs nothing.

## The two deadlines

Both decide whether your complaint is even looked at.

<Callout type="rule" title="30 days before, one year after" cite="Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021" citeUrl="https://cms.rbi.org.in">
You may file **after 30 days** have passed since you complained to the bank in
writing, if it did not reply or you are not satisfied with the reply. You must
file **within one year** of receiving the bank's reply — or within one year and
30 days of your original complaint, if the bank never replied.
</Callout>

File on day 20 and the complaint is returned as premature. File 13 months after
the bank's rejection letter and it is time-barred. Note the date you first
complained; it is the hinge the whole thing turns on.

## What it covers

The 2021 scheme merged three earlier schemes into one, so the coverage is wide:
banks, NBFCs, and digital payment system participants. In practice the complaints
it handles well are the ones where a rule was broken and the facts are documented:

- Unauthorised transactions where the bank refused the refund
- Failed transactions not reversed inside the RBI turn-around time
- Credit information errors not corrected within 30 days
- Card closure not processed within seven working days
- Charges levied without notice, or contrary to the sanctioned terms
- Non-adherence to a published fair practices code

<Callout type="warn" title="What it will not do">
The Ombudsman does not review a bank's **commercial judgement**. If a loan was
rejected because the bank did not like your profile, that is its call to make
and the Ombudsman will not substitute its own. The complaint has to be that a
rule, a published policy or an agreed term was breached — not that the outcome
was unfair.
</Callout>

It will also not take a complaint that is already before a court or a consumer
forum on the same cause of action.

## Filing it

The portal is `cms.rbi.org.in`. Registration is by mobile number and OTP.

What makes a complaint succeed is unglamorous: dates, references, and the bank's
own words. Upload the original complaint, the bank's reply, the transaction
record, and anything the bank put in writing. A complaint that says "the bank
behaved badly" goes nowhere. One that says "I reported on 4 March, the bank
acknowledged on 4 March with reference X, the circular required a credit within
10 working days, the credit has not arrived" resolves quickly.

Name the specific instrument if there is one. Several pages on this site give you
the circular number for exactly this reason.

## What happens next

Most complaints are closed by **facilitation**: the Ombudsman puts the question to
the bank, the bank re-examines it, and it is settled. A meaningful proportion of
complaints are resolved the moment a bank realises an external body is now
looking.

If facilitation fails, the Ombudsman may pass an **award** directing the bank to
remedy the issue and to pay compensation for your actual loss, plus a separate
amount for mental agony and harassment. If your complaint is rejected, you have
30 days to appeal to the Appellate Authority at RBI.

<Callout type="ok" title="Nobody can file it better than you">
Agents who offer to file for a fee add nothing. The form asks for your facts and
your documents, and you have both. If an agent promises a guaranteed outcome,
that is a sign to walk away — nobody can guarantee an Ombudsman result.
</Callout>

## If the Ombudsman is not the right route

Where the complaint is about a service deficiency causing you loss, and the
Ombudsman has rejected it or it falls outside the scheme, a **consumer commission**
is the next step. That is slower, may involve a fee, and is worth it mainly for
larger amounts. The Ombudsman is free and fast; try it first.
