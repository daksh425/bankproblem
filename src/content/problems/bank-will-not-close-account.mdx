Closing an account is a one-form job. It drags because a closed account is a lost
relationship, and branches are measured on relationships.

## Clear the ground first

Do these before you walk in, because each one is a legitimate reason to refuse and
each one is on you:

- **Remove standing instructions and mandates.** A live mandate on an account being closed is a bounce waiting to happen. [How to withdraw one properly](/payments/standing-instruction-not-cancelled).
- **Move salary, pension and refund credits** to the new account. An inward credit to a closed account bounces back to the sender.
- **Settle any negative balance**, including accrued minimum balance penalties.
- **Surrender the unused cheque leaves and the debit card.** Banks are entitled to ask for these back.
- **Decide where the closing balance goes** — transfer to another account is cleaner than cash.

<Callout type="warn" title="Check for penalties before you go in">
A low-balance account often has penalties accrued against it. Those have to be
settled to close, and if they were levied improperly you want to dispute them
first rather than paying to get out. [What banks may actually
charge](/accounts/minimum-balance-penalty-rules).
</Callout>

## What they may not do

- **Insist you keep it open.** A retention pitch is fine; refusing to process the form is not.
- **Require a reason.** You are entitled to close an account without explaining why.
- **Lose the form.** Submit it, and get an acknowledgement with a date and a reference number. Without that you have no proof the clock ever started.
- **Levy a charge not in the published schedule.** Ask which schedule entry a fee comes from.

## What to collect

Ask for a **closure confirmation in writing**, and a **final statement** showing a
nil balance and the closure date.

Then check in a month that the account no longer shows anywhere it should not,
particularly if it was linked to a loan or a credit card. Accounts "closed" in the
branch system but still live elsewhere are common.

## If it still has not happened

Written complaint to the grievance redressal officer: the account number, the date
you submitted the closure request, the reference number, and what you want, which
is closure plus written confirmation.

One page.
[The structure to copy](/disputes/writing-a-bank-complaint-that-works).

Thirty days later the Ombudsman will take up a bank's failure to act on a valid
request under its own process.
[How to file](/disputes/rbi-ombudsman-how-to-file).

## The alternative worth considering

If the only problem is the minimum balance, you may not need to close anything.
Every bank must offer a Basic Savings Bank Deposit account with **no minimum
balance**, and you can ask to convert the existing account rather than closing it.
That keeps the account history, which has some value, and removes the charge.
[How BSBD accounts work](/accounts/minimum-balance-penalty-rules).

And do not simply abandon the account. An ignored account accrues penalties, then
goes dormant.
[What dormancy means](/accounts/dormant-account-reactivation).
