Card acquisition in India runs on telesales, and telesales runs on promises. The
promise that most often fails to survive the first renewal is "lifetime free".

## Get the evidence before you argue

Three things count, in descending order of strength:

1. **The welcome kit or the most important terms and conditions document** you received at issuance. If it names the fee as nil, the argument is already over.
2. **Email or SMS from the issuer** at the time of application describing the offer.
3. **The sales call recording.** Card sales calls are recorded. You can ask the bank to retrieve the call for a specific date and number. Banks resist, but a written request naming the date is a concrete ask that is hard to brush off.

What does not count: what the agent told you, remembered a year later, with no
date.

<Callout type="warn" title="Pay it, then dispute it">
An annual fee you refuse to pay does not stay a disputed fee. It becomes an
overdue balance, attracts interest and a late payment charge, and is reported to
the credit bureaus. Pay it, write "paid under protest, reversal requested" in
your complaint, and pursue it separately. Protecting your credit report is worth
more than the fee.
</Callout>

## Spend-based waivers are a different thing

Many cards are not lifetime free at all; they waive the fee if you spend above a
threshold in the card year. If that is your card, check two details before
complaining:

- **Which year.** The waiver is usually assessed on the preceding card anniversary year, not the calendar year.
- **Which spends count.** Fuel, rent payments, wallet loads, EMI conversions and cash withdrawals are commonly excluded from the qualifying total. People miss the threshold by spending heavily on exactly the categories that do not count.

If you crossed the threshold on qualifying spends and were still charged, that is
a straightforward billing error and the reversal should be quick.

## Escalating

Write to the grievance redressal officer with the fee date, the amount, and your
evidence attached. Ask for a reversal and a written explanation if refused. Keep
it to one page.

Thirty days after that complaint, if it is unresolved or refused, the RBI
Ombudsman covers credit card grievances including charges levied contrary to the
agreed terms. It is free.
[How to file](/disputes/rbi-ombudsman-how-to-file), and
[how to write the complaint so it is not template-replied](/disputes/writing-a-bank-complaint-that-works).

## If you decide to close the card instead

Clear the balance first, including the disputed fee, then request closure in
writing. The issuer has seven working days and owes you ₹500 a day beyond that.
[What to do when it drags](/cards/credit-card-closure-not-processed).
