I started BankProblem because almost everything written about an Indian banking
problem is published by someone with something to sell: a bank explaining its own
process, a fintech collecting a lead, an aggregator chasing impressions. Those
pages describe how things are supposed to work. Very few tell you what to do when
they do not.

I am not a banker, a chartered accountant or a lawyer, and I do not pretend to be.
What I do instead is show the working. Every rule stated on this site is traced
back to the RBI circular, master direction or scheme that contains it, and that
source is listed at the foot of the page with its date, so you can read the
original rather than trust me.

Where a page touches tax or anything that genuinely needs a professional, it says
so and tells you to go and get one. Where a problem has no clean fix, it says that
too, rather than padding the page.

If something here is wrong or has gone out of date, I would rather hear it than
not. [Tell me](/contact) and I will correct it on the page, visibly.
